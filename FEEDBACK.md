# Feedback

We'd love to hear from you. Head over to
[github.com/gyrforge/gyrseek/discussions](https://github.com/gyrforge/gyrseek/discussions)
to give feedback about the product or request features.

## Guidelines

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagreement is fine; personal attacks are not.
- Stay on topic — keep discussions relevant to gyrseek.
- Search before posting to avoid duplicates.
- No spam, trolling, or self-promotion.
